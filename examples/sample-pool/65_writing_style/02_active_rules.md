# Active style rules (G###)

Rules other skills must follow when writing on the person's behalf. Priority: rules from corrections (`Origin: K###`) > higher Strength > rules from patterns. Ignore rules whose status is `replaced`. Format:

```
### G### — <short title>
- Scope: <CV-EN | Email | all>
- Rule: <clear imperative sentence>
- DO: "<literal example>"
- AVOID: "<literal example>"
- Origin: <pattern [S###] | correction K###>
- Strength: <n>
- Status: active | replaced by G###
```

---

### G001 — Numbers early in the bullet
- Scope: CV-EN
- Rule: Put the before → after numbers in the first half of the bullet.
- DO: "Reduced the nightly route optimisation from 5 hours to 40 minutes by batching DB writes."
- AVOID: "Worked on improving the performance of several nightly jobs."
- Origin: pattern [S001]
- Strength: 1
- Status: active
